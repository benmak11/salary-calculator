Features

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Incomatic ("the app," "we," "our") is a take-home pay calculator for iOS. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Location

Incomatic requests access to your device's location, with your permission, solely to detect your work state and pre-fill it in the calculator. Location data is used momentarily on your device to determine your state and is not stored, logged, or transmitted to our servers beyond what's needed to complete that lookup. You can decline location access and select your state manually instead.

Financial and payroll information you enter

To calculate your take-home pay, the app sends the figures you provide (earnings, pay frequency, filing status, W-4 details, state/county, pre-tax benefit elections, and retirement contribution percentages) to our calculation backend so it can compute your paycheck breakdown and return it to the app.

  • If you are not signed in, the calculation is processed to produce your result and is not saved to any personal history. Our server logs record only non-identifying metadata about a request (such as the country, state, tax year, and pay frequency, and whether a bonus or hourly input was included) and deliberately do not record the salary or dollar amounts you enter, or the full contents of your request.
  • If you sign in (see "Account information"), the calculation you run (the inputs and the computed results) is saved to your private calculation history so you can revisit it later. You can delete any saved calculation from within the app at any time.

Account information

Signing in is optional and is used only to save and sync your calculation history; the calculator is fully usable without an account. Incomatic uses Sign in with Apple, so we never receive or handle your Apple password. When you sign in, our backend stores a record for your account containing your Apple user identifier, the display name you choose to share (Apple provides this only on your first sign-in), and account timestamps (when the account was created and last used). We do not store your email address. Sign in with Apple lets you keep it private (including via Apple's "Hide My Email" relay), and our backend does not retain it. Your saved calculations are stored privately against your Apple user identifier and are only accessible when you are signed in.

Budget goals and expenses

If you choose to build a budget, the app collects the savings goals and expenses you enter (name, amount, cadence, category, and any target date) along with the take-home figures from your calculation, so it can produce a paycheck-by-paycheck plan. This information is stored against your account like your calculation history, and you can edit or delete it in the app at any time. Generating a plan requires an AI service operated by Google; see "Budgeting and AI" below.

App usage and diagnostics

To understand which parts of the app are actually used, Incomatic records a small set of usage events, such as starting a session, completing a step of onboarding, finishing a calculation, and setting or dismissing the payday reminder. These are our own records; no third-party analytics SDK is involved.

  • What each event contains. The event name, when it happened, the app version, and a short set of labels, for example your state, filing status, pay frequency, and whether you were signed in.
  • Amounts are recorded as ranges, never as figures. Take-home pay is reduced to a $1,000-wide band such as "2000-3000" before it leaves your device, and our backend rejects any event that tries to send a decimal amount. Your salary is never part of an event.
  • A random per-install identifier. Events carry an identifier the app generates for itself on first launch. It is not your device's advertising identifier and is not shared with anyone; deleting and reinstalling the app produces a new one. If you are signed in, events also carry your account identifier so activity can be joined across your devices.
  • This happens whether or not you sign in. Signing in is optional, so measuring only signed-in users would tell us about a self-selected minority rather than how the app is really used.

These events are kept until we no longer need them for product measurement; we have not set a fixed deletion schedule. Deleting your account removes your account record, saved calculations, grants and budget, but does not retroactively remove usage events already recorded. If you would like those removed as well, contact us and we will delete them.

Budgeting and AI

Incomatic's budgeting feature uses Google's generative AI (Gemini) to draft your plan. To do that, the values you have entered (your take-home pay, your expenses, and your savings goals, including the amounts you typed in) are sent to Google's AI service through our backend. Your name, email, and Apple user identifier are not included.

  • This only happens if you explicitly allow it. Before anything is sent, the app shows a consent step describing this in plain language; if you choose "Not now," no budget data is sent to Google and the feature is not used.
  • The AI's plan is checked against a deterministic budgeting engine that runs on your device, which also serves as a fallback if the AI service is unavailable.
  • Google's handling of the data it receives is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy for its AI services. We do not permit this data to be used to train Google's models.
  • Every other part of Incomatic (the calculator, Insights, history, and RSU tracking) works without any AI service, and none of your data is sent to Google for those features.

Information We Do Not Collect

Incomatic does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not collect your contact list or device advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. The usage events described under "App usage and diagnostics" are our own and stay with us; they are not sent to an analytics vendor and are not used to build an advertising profile.

How Information Is Used

Financial figures you enter are used exclusively to generate the paycheck breakdown displayed in the app and, if you are signed in, to save that breakdown to your personal history. Budget goals and expenses are used exclusively to build and display your budget plan, including the AI generation step you consent to. Location is used exclusively to pre-fill your work state. Account information is used exclusively to authenticate you and associate your saved calculations with your account.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your inputs are transmitted to our own calculation backend over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The one exception is the budgeting feature: with your explicit consent, your budget figures are sent to Google's AI service to generate your plan, as described in "Budgeting and AI" above. Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple; your use of it is also governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Data Security

All communication between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit via HTTPS. In addition, the salary and dollar amounts you enter are never written to our server logs, and (when you are not signed in) your requests are not associated with any persistent user identifier. Saved calculations are stored privately and are only returned to your own authenticated account.

Children's Privacy

Incomatic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings; the app will fall back to manual state selection.
  • You can use the calculator without signing in, in which case no calculations or account data are stored.
  • You can decline the budgeting consent step, or simply not use budgeting. The rest of the app is unaffected and nothing is sent to Google's AI. Deleting a goal or expense removes it from our backend.
  • If you have signed in, you can delete individual saved calculations at any time from the History screen, which removes them from our backend.
  • You can permanently delete your account at any time from the account screen (tap your profile, then Delete Account). This erases your account record and all of your saved calculations, RSU grants and budget from our backend and cannot be undone. Usage events recorded before deletion are not removed automatically; email us and we will delete those too.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app's features change. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
